Dear Rai,

Hello! I know this letter is a little corny, but I guess I just felt like writing something for you. Sometimes, there are things that are easier to express through written words, and this is one of those times.

I may not always know what's going on in your life, what thoughts keep you up at night, or what battles you're quietly dealing with. I won't pretend that I understand everything you're going through, but I hope you know that you never have to figure everything out on your own. Whatever happens, I'll always be on your side, okay?

I hope you never feel like you have to hide your struggles just because you don't want to bother anyone. You don't have to be okay all the time, and you certainly don't have to put on a brave face whenever things get difficult. If you ever need someone to listen, rant to, distract you, or simply sit with you through the silence, you know where to find me.

And please, don't forget that I'm always here beside you, even when I'm not physically there. Distance, busy schedules, and the little inconveniences of life won't change the fact that you have someone rooting for you. I might not always be able to show up exactly when you need me, but I'll always try my best to remind you that you're not alone.

I'm genuinely grateful that life allowed our paths to cross. Out of all the people we could've met, somehow, we ended up becoming friends. And honestly, I wouldn't trade that for anything. Thank you for being someone I can call my best friend, for all the conversations, random moments, laughter, and even the ordinary days that somehow become memorable because you're part of them.

I hope life treats you gently, Rai. I hope you find reasons to smile even on the days when everything feels a little too much. I hope you give yourself enough patience, kindness, and understanding, especially when things don't go according to plan.

And whenever you forget how much you matter, let this letter be a little reminder that someone out here is proud of you, believes in you, and wishes nothing but good things for you.

Take care of yourself, always. You've got a friend in me, through the good days, the difficult ones, and everything in between.

With love and appreciation,
Marchel
